# MapLink Pro Version Support Lifecycle

## Introduction
MapLink Pro 11 brought the product up-to-date and made it better prepared for modern software development where new security issues are being discovered constantly requiring regular updates to all software solutions. The version gave us the foundation to develop the SDKs’ capabilities and respond better to our user's evolving requirements.

As we move forward and to help our users plan to keep their systems up-to-date and resilient, we are formalising our version support lifecycle which is designed to align with market requirements and cyber resilience guidelines (e.g. The EU's Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) which comes fully into force in December 2027).

## Timeline
We are introducing a 2+2+1 version support lifecycle, starting in November 2027. At this time, the following will happen:
- MapLink Pro 12 will be released into the new General Availability phase.
- MapLink Pro 11 will enter the new Extended Support phase.
- MapLink Pro 10 will enter retirement.
Each version from MapLink 11 onwards will follow the 2+2+1 pattern.

| MapLink Pro Major Version | General Availability Begins | GA Ends | Extended Support Ends | Mature Phase Ends | Retired |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| 10 | - | - | - | - | November 2027 |
| 11 | Feb 2025 | Nov 2027 | Nov 2029 | Nov 2030 | Nov 2030 |
| 12 | Nov 2027 | Nov 2029 | Nov 2031 | Nov 2032 | Nov 2032 |
| 13 | Nov 2029 | Nov 2031 | Nov 2033 | Nov 2034 | Nov 2034 |

> MapLink Pro 11 has a longer General Availability phase than later versions. This is because the lifecycle was not formalised at the time of its release and gives users a full 12 months between announcement of the lifecycle timelines and the version entering Extended Support.

## Support Coverage
The support coverage of each phase in a version’s lifecycle is documented in the following table.

| | General Availability | Extended Support | Mature | Retired |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Technical support** | Yes | Yes | Reasonable efforts | No |
| **Online reference** | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| **Bug fixes** | Yes | Critical only | No | No |
| **Security updates** | Yes | Critical only | Critical only | No |
| **New features** | Yes | No | No | No |
| **Support for new platforms** | Yes | No | No | No |
| **New licences** | List price | List price +15% | Not sold | Not sold |
| **Support & maintenance renewal** | List price | Same as GA | Same as Extended | None * |

<small>* Bespoke support if required, Price on Application (POA).</small>
